Edge

A personal app for one trader.

Edge keeps a trading journal, a calendar, notes, habits and money in one place. It is built and used by one person and is not offered to the public.

Google Calendar

Edge connects to its owner's Google Calendar to show events and to save the events created or changed in Edge. The data stays on the owner's computer.
